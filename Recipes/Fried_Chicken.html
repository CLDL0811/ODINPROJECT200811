Fried Chicken

Description:

Filipino Fried Chicken takes the familiar idea of fried chicken and gives it our own local twist. Instead of buttermilk or heavy breading, we use a marinade made with soy sauce, calamansi, and seasonings that soak into the meat to give it flavor from the inside out. The coating stays thin and crispy, made with flour and cornstarch that create a beautiful golden crust. It is not just food for the stomach; it is food for the soul. Even with the cold weather outside, the scent of soy and garlic always made the place feel like home.

Filipino Fried Chicken has been a favorite in many Filipino homes for generations. It is one of those dishes that can make any meal feel special, even on an ordinary day. Growing up, fried chicken was always a treat for me especially the Jollibee Fried Chicken which is the most commonly known Filipino Fried Chicken. The smell of it cooking in the kitchen could pull everyone to the table long before it was served. It is simple, flavorful, and always satisfying.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken
  • Flour
  • Oil
  • Soy Sauce
  • Calamansi
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Seasoning
  • Constarch

    • Optional:

  • Crispy Mix

Steps to cooking:

  1. Prepare the Marinating sauce, just simply mixed your Soy Sauce and a little bit of salt, pepper, and your preffered seasoning
  2. Marinade your Chicken with the sauce and leave it either overnight or over 2-3 hours in the fridge, it is reccommended that you let it marinate overnight to guarantee that the chicken has soaked up the flavour of the marinade sace
  3. Meanwhile before you take the chicken out of the fridge prepare the dry ingredients for couting, which is simply a mixture of flour and cornstarch with a little bit of groud pepper and seasoning of your own choice.
  4. While waiting for it to cool, prepare a separate plate and prepare either your flour, constrach, and/or crispy mix or simply buy a crispy mix. You can mix those dry Ingredients.
  5. After the preparation, get the marinated chicken and coat it with the dry mix and after thouroughly coating the chicken let it set to a plate.
  6. Pre-heat the Pan, and after it steams put your preffered oil, in this step it requires a lot of oil just sure that it covers the whole chicken but if you're saving for oil you can just put an amount of oil where the half is submerged in oil and simply flip it.
  7. Upon noticing the changing of color on the chicken you simply flip it with the use of your tongs or as we like to call it clip and flip the other side.
  8. Cook rice while the chicken is cooking. Some reccommends to cock/fry the chicken twice to guarantee that the meat is cooked and not raw
  9. Upon finishing your batch, simply eat it and enjoy.
