Pancit Lomi

Description:

Lomi or Pancit Lomi is a type of noodle soup dish that makes use of thick egg noodles. Although this dish originated from the Chinese, several different regional variations became available throughout the years.

Pancit Lomi is a hearty and tasty soup made with fresh egg noodles, chicken, and vegetables. It's easy to make in under an hour and delicious and filling as a midday snack or dinner meal. There are many variations of Lomi what we will be making is a personal recipe of a kind of Lomi, although it is somewhat inspired from various kinds of lomis that you can see from the internet.

Ingredients:

  • Any kind of Meat
  • Fish Sauce
  • Oil
  • Carrots
  • Any kind of Cabbage
  • Pancit or Lomi Noodles
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Seasoning
  • Constarch

    • Optional:

  • Soy Sauce
  • Eggs

Steps to cooking:

  1. Prepare the Ingredients, Chop the carrots to small strips, including the cabbage, cut the onion and garlic into diced, cut your choice meat into either small chunks or big chunks. And you can marinate it or not. For the conrstarch put in water and simply mix
  2. Pre-heat the huge pan and after a bit of steam put the oil, after the oil is set in 2 minutes, you can put the diced onion and garlic.
  3. Upon the darkeing of the garlic and union make sure that it is not burnt, put the chicken in your salt and pepper and let it saute. Make sure to stir it every 30 seconds so that it doesn't stick on your pan.
  4. After making sure that your chicken is cooked and has a brownish color you can add your vegetables the carrots first.
  5. After sauting it for a minute put a tiny bit of fish sauce and then pour just the right amount of water.
  6. In this step will occur 2 ways depending on what kind of noodle you prefer, if you want the noodles being fully cooked or over cooked you can add the noodles before the water boils, but if you prefer having them half cooked or just cooked, you can add it after the water boils along with the cabbage .
  7. during the cooking process the water constrach should be mixed along with your noodles, so if you prefer it getting cooked before the water boils simply put pour your water cornstarch along with the noodles, otherwise put it on the water after it boild along with the noodles and cabbage.
  8. The cabbage will always be the last one to be put on the soup since it might be over cooked if it were put on the pan early
  9. For the Last step you can simply adjust and add your ingredients and seasoning again until the Lomi's soup is suited to your taste.
  10. Cook rice while waiting for the water to boil.
  11. Upon finishing your adjustment, simply serve, eat it and enjoy.
