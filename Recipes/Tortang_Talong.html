Tortang Talong

Description:

As a Filipino dish Tortang Talong is usually served with steamed rice, though it also works having it with garlic fried rice too and dip it to a sauce. This Filipino eggplant omelet is made by simply grilling the eggplant whole, peeling off the charred skin, flattening it with a fork, and frying it with beaten egg. You only need eggplant, eggs, salt, and cooking oil.

This Filipino Food is commonly eaten in the Philippines, its name describes the dish plainly. Torta means omelet or flat cake, while talong is the Filipino word for eggplant. Some people shorten the whole thing to tortalong. In English it usually shows up on menus as an eggplant omelet.

Ingredients:

  • Eggplant
  • Egg
  • Flour
  • Oil
  • Salt

    • Optional:

  • Onion
  • Pepper
  • Crispy Mix or;
  • Constarch

Steps to cooking:

    Note: that this is usually done with just the use of our hands and only uses some of the kitchen equipment

  1. Cook your eggplant whole in the grill or simply on your stove although it is much more reccomended to grill it. Do Not remove its stem, that's where you can control on grilling the vegetable
  2. While the eggplant is getting charged, we need to prepare the egg mixture, which is usually only 1-2 eggs and salt but you can add diced onions, peppers, and any other seasoning that might suit your taste.
  3. After making sure that all of the skin are charred let it rest for a few 3-5 minutes to let it coolafter cooling pell all of it except the stem.
  4. While waiting for it to cool, prepare a separate plate and prepare either your flour, constrach, and/or crispy mix. You can mix those dry Ingredients.
  5. After the preparation, get the eggplant and dip it on your egg mixture and flatten it with the use of a fork.
  6. After the dip on the egg mixutre you raise it and start coating it on the dry ingredients make sure to coat it whole.
  7. Pre-heat the Pan, and after it steams put your preffered oil, in this step it requires only a little oil just make sure that it covers the whole surface where the eggplant is being cooked.
  8. Upon noticing the changing of color on the eggplant you simply flip it with the use of you hands on the stem.
  9. Cook rice while the eggplant is cooking.
  10. Upon finishing your batch, simply eat it and enjoy.
